# 💼 Sole Trader Tax Tracker

A mobile-first progressive web app designed to help sole traders manage their finances, track expenses, and calculate tax obligations throughout the year.

## ✨ Features

### 📊 Dashboard
- Real-time overview of today's income, expenses, and net profit
- Weekly, monthly, and yearly summaries
- Quick access to key financial metrics
- Tax liability calculations

### 🚗 Mileage Tracking
- Odometer start/end logging
- Automatic MPG calculation
- Total miles tracking
- Cost per mile calculator
- Future maintenance cost estimates (wear & tear)
- Business mileage deductions

### 💰 Expense Management
- Categorized expense tracking:
  - ⛽ Fuel
  - 🍔 Food
  - 🔧 Vehicle Maintenance
  - 🛠️ Tools
  - 👔 Work Clothes
  - 🛡️ Insurance
  - 📱 Phone/Internet
  - 🏢 Office Supplies
  - 📦 Other
- Expense breakdown by category
- Recurring expense tracking
- Daily expense deduction from income

### 💵 Income Tracking
- Simple income logging
- Net daily wage calculator (income minus expenses)
- Daily to yearly income tallying
- Client/job descriptions

### 📈 Tax Calculator
- Automatic UK tax calculations:
  - Personal Allowance (£12,570)
  - Income Tax (20% basic rate)
  - National Insurance (9%)
- Daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly tax estimates
- Business expense offsetting
- Annual tax liability overview

### 📱 Mobile-First Design
- Responsive layout optimized for phones
- Progressive Web App (PWA) - installable on mobile devices
- Touch-friendly interface
- Works offline with local storage

### 🔄 Data Management
- Local storage (all data stays on your device)
- Export data to JSON
- Import data from backup
- Clear all data option

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Installation

1. **Open the app** in your mobile browser
2. **Add to Home Screen**:
   - **iOS**: Tap the Share button → "Add to Home Screen"
   - **Android**: Tap menu → "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App"

### First Steps

1. **Set up your first income entry** in the Income tab
2. **Add daily expenses** as they occur
3. **Track mileage** for business trips
4. **Check the Dashboard** for daily summaries
5. **Review the Reports tab** for tax calculations

## 📖 Usage Guide

### Recording Mileage

1. Navigate to the **Mileage** tab
2. Enter your **starting odometer reading**
3. Enter your **ending odometer reading**
4. (Optional) Add **fuel added** for MPG calculation
5. Select the **date**
6. Click **Add Mileage Entry**

The app automatically calculates:
- Miles driven
- MPG (if fuel added)
- Total miles to date
- Cost per mile
- Future maintenance costs

### Adding Expenses

1. Go to the **Expenses** tab
2. Select an **expense category**
3. Enter the **amount** in GBP
4. Add a **description**
5. Select the **date**
6. Check **recurring** if applicable
7. Click **Add Expense**

### Recording Income

1. Open the **Income** tab
2. Enter the **amount earned**
3. Add a **description** (client/job)
4. Select the **date**
5. Click **Add Income**

The app shows your net daily wage after deducting today's expenses.

### Understanding Tax Calculations

The **Reports** tab shows:

- **Gross Income**: Total money earned
- **Business Expenses**: All tracked expenses
- **Taxable Profit**: Income minus expenses
- **Tax Breakdown**:
  - Personal Allowance: First £12,570 is tax-free
  - Income Tax: 20% on profit above allowance
  - National Insurance: 9% on profit above £12,570
- **Time-based estimates**: Daily/weekly/monthly tax amounts

## 💡 Tips

1. **Record expenses daily** - Don't let them pile up
2. **Keep descriptions clear** - Future you will thank you
3. **Track all business mileage** - It's tax-deductible
4. **Review reports weekly** - Stay on top of your tax obligations
5. **Export your data monthly** - Keep backups just in case
6. **Separate business and personal** - Only track business-related items

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

- **All data is stored locally** on your device
- **No server uploads** - your data never leaves your device
- **No account required** - no sign-ups or passwords
- **Export/Import** - you control your data backups

## 🛠️ Technical Details

- **Pure JavaScript** - No frameworks required
- **Local Storage API** - Persistent data storage
- **Progressive Web App** - Installable and offline-capable
- **Responsive CSS** - Mobile-first design
- **No dependencies** - Fast and lightweight

## 📊 UK Tax Information (2025/2026)

- Personal Allowance: £12,570
- Basic Rate Tax: 20% (£12,571 - £50,270)
- National Insurance: 9% (above £12,570)

*Note: This app provides estimates. Always consult with an accountant for official tax advice.*

## 🆘 Support

For issues or questions:
1. Check that you're using a modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge)
2. Ensure JavaScript is enabled
3. Try exporting and re-importing your data
4. Clear browser cache and reload
