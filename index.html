📚 UK Tax & National Insurance Guide

🔵 National Insurance Classes

Class 1 NI (Employees - PAYE)

• Paid by employees earning over £12,570/year

• Deducted automatically from salary by employer

• Rate: 8% on £12,570 - £50,270, then 2% above

• Qualifies you for State Pension and benefits

Class 2 NI (Self-Employed - Low Earners)

• Below £6,725 profit: OPTIONAL payment (£3.45/week) to qualify for State Pension

• Above £6,725 profit: Automatically qualify for State Pension - NO Class 2 NI charge

✓ This app sets Class 2 NI to £0 if you're above the threshold

Class 3 NI (Voluntary Contributions)

• Optional payments to fill gaps in your NI record

• £17.45 per week (2025/26)

• Useful if you have incomplete years affecting State Pension

Class 4 NI (Self-Employed - Profit-Based)

• Paid on self-employment profits above £12,570

• 9% on profits between £12,570 - £50,270

• 2% on profits above £50,270

⚠️ Does NOT count towards State Pension (use Class 2 or 3 for that)

💰 Income Tax Thresholds (2025/26)

Personal Allowance

• £12,570 - Tax-free earnings

• Reduced by £1 for every £2 earned over £100,000

Basic Rate (20%)

• £12,571 - £50,270

• Most people pay 20% on earnings in this band

Higher Rate (40%)

• £50,271 - £125,140

• 40% tax on earnings in this band

Additional Rate (45%)

• £125,141+

• 45% tax on earnings above this threshold

📋 HMRC Allowable Deductions

You can deduct these business expenses to reduce your taxable profit:

Vehicle & Travel

• Mileage Allowance: 45p/mile (first 10,000), then 25p/mile

• OR Actual Costs: Fuel, insurance, repairs, MOT (business use %)

⚠️ You cannot claim both!

• Public transport, parking, tolls

Office & Equipment

• Office supplies, stationery, postage

• Computer equipment, software, phones

• Tools and equipment for your trade

Professional Services

• Accountant and bookkeeping fees

• Professional insurance and memberships

• Legal fees (business-related)

Working from Home

• Simplified: £6/week (25-50 hrs), £18/week (51-100 hrs), £26/week (101+ hrs)

• OR Actual costs: Portion of rent, utilities, internet (business use %)

Marketing & Advertising

• Website hosting and domain costs

• Online advertising (Google, Facebook, etc.)

• Business cards, flyers, promotional materials

Training & Development

• Courses to update existing skills

• Industry-specific qualifications

⚠️ Cannot claim for new skills unrelated to current business

Other Allowable Expenses

• Bank charges and transaction fees

• Phone and internet (business use %)

• Clothing (protective/branded uniforms only)

• Stock and raw materials

❌ NOT Allowable

• Entertainment costs (client meals, events)

• Normal commuting costs (home to regular workplace)

• Personal clothing (even if worn for work)

• Personal expenses or fines
